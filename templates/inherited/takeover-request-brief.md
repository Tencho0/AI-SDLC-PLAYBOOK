# Takeover Request Brief

> Produced at intake of an existing-system takeover to decide whether and how to proceed. Save the filled copy to `src/<engagement>/delivery/`.

## 1. System summary
_What the existing system is and what it does._

## 2. Why client needs help
_The reason the client is handing over or seeking support._

## 3. Current pain points
_The problems the client is experiencing today._

## 4. Business criticality
_How important the system is to the client's business._

## 5. Known urgent issues
_Issues that need attention immediately._

## 6. Unknowns
_What is still unclear about the system and the request._

## 7. Initial risks
_Early commercial, technical, and delivery risks._

## 8. Required access
_Access and resources needed to assess and work on the system._

## 9. Suggested assessment approach
_The recommended way to evaluate the system before committing._
