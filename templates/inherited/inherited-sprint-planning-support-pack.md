# Inherited Sprint Planning Support Pack

> Produced to support Sprint Planning for an inherited system where regression risk drives sequencing. Save the filled copy to `src/<engagement>/delivery/`.

## 1. Candidate items
_The backlog items considered for the Sprint._

## 2. Business value
_The value each item delivers to the client._

## 3. Stabilization value
_How much each item improves system stability._

## 4. Technical risk
_The technical risk of taking on each item._

## 5. Regression risk
_The risk each item poses to existing behavior._

## 6. Dependencies
_Dependencies between items and on external factors._

## 7. Suggested Sprint Goal options
_Possible Sprint Goals for the team to choose from._

## 8. Suggested safe sequence
_An order of work that minimizes risk._

## 9. Testing effort
_The testing expected for the candidate work._

## 10. Sprint confidence level
_How confident the plan looks given the unknowns._
