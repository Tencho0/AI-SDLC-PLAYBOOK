# Refined Story Pack

> Produced during backlog refinement to make a story Ready for Sprint Planning. Save the filled copy to `src/<engagement>/delivery/`.

## 1. Story summary
_The user story in one or two sentences (role, need, outcome)._

## 2. Business value
_Why this story matters and the value it delivers._

## 3. Acceptance criteria
_Testable conditions that define when the story is satisfied._

## 4. Dependencies
_Other stories, systems, or decisions this story depends on._

## 5. Edge cases
_Boundary and exceptional scenarios to account for._

## 6. Technical notes
_Implementation considerations, constraints, or approach hints._

## 7. QA notes
_Testing considerations and quality concerns for this story._

## 8. Risks
_Delivery, technical, or business risks tied to this story._

## 9. Open questions
_Unresolved questions that need answers before or during the Sprint._

## 10. Definition of Ready status
_Whether the story meets the Definition of Ready, with any gaps._
