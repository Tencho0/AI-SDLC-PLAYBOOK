# AI PR Review Report

> Produced as a first-pass review before human PR review and merge. Save the filled copy to `src/<engagement>/delivery/`.

_What the pull request changes, at a glance._

## 2. Acceptance criteria coverage
_How well the change satisfies the linked acceptance criteria._

## 3. Missing tests
_Test coverage gaps the change should address._

## 4. Risky areas
_Parts of the change that warrant extra scrutiny._

## 5. Security concerns
_Potential security issues introduced by the change._

## 6. Architecture concerns
_Design or structural concerns raised by the change._

## 7. Regression risks
_Existing behavior that the change might break._

## 8. Suggested improvements
_Optional improvements to quality, clarity, or maintainability._

## 9. Documentation needs
_Documentation that should accompany the change._

## 10. Human reviewer checklist
_Items the human reviewer must confirm before approving._
