# Security Review Report

> Produced when reviewing code, architecture, or configuration for security risks. Save the filled copy to `src/<engagement>/delivery/`.

## 1. Scope reviewed
_What code, architecture, and configuration were in scope._

## 2. Authentication & authorization findings
_Issues with identity, sessions, roles, and access control._

## 3. Input validation & injection risks
_Untrusted-input handling and injection vulnerabilities._

## 4. Secrets & credential handling
_How secrets, keys, and credentials are stored and used._

## 5. Dependency & supply-chain risks
_Risks from third-party libraries and build artifacts._

## 6. Configuration & infrastructure risks
_Misconfigurations in environments, services, and infrastructure._

## 7. Data protection & privacy
_Handling of sensitive and personal data, at rest and in transit._

## 8. Severity-ranked findings
_Findings ordered by severity and impact._

## 9. Remediation recommendations
_Recommended fixes and mitigations for each finding._

## 10. Residual risks & open questions
_Risks that remain and questions needing human decisions._
