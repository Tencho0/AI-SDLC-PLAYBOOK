# Retrospective Insights Pack

> Produced for the Sprint Retrospective to surface patterns and improvement actions. Save the filled copy to `src/<engagement>/delivery/`.

## 1. What went well
_Positives worth keeping and reinforcing._

## 2. What did not go well
_Problems and frustrations from the Sprint._

## 3. Recurring blockers
_Impediments that keep reappearing across Sprints._

## 4. Process issues
_Workflow or ceremony issues that slowed delivery._

## 5. Quality issues
_Defects, rework, or quality gaps observed._

## 6. Communication issues
_Breakdowns in team or stakeholder communication._

## 7. AI usage observations
_How AI helped or hindered, and where to adjust its use._

## 8. Suggested improvements
_Candidate improvement experiments for the team._

## 9. Action items for next Sprint
_Concrete, owned actions to carry into the next Sprint._
