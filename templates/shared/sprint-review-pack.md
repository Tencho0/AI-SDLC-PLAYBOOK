# Sprint Review Pack

> Produced ahead of the Sprint Review to present the Increment and gather feedback. Save the filled copy to `src/<engagement>/delivery/`.

## 1. Sprint Goal summary
_The Sprint Goal and how well it was met._

## 2. Completed Product Backlog items
_The items finished and accepted this Sprint._

## 3. Demo flow
_The sequence to demonstrate the Increment to stakeholders._

## 4. Known limitations
_Limitations or incomplete aspects of the Increment._

## 5. Stakeholder feedback
_Feedback captured during or after the review._

## 6. Decisions made
_Decisions reached during the Sprint Review._

## 7. New backlog items
_New items surfaced from the review._

## 8. Scope changes
_Changes to scope or priority arising from the review._

## 9. Follow-up actions
_Actions to take after the Sprint Review._
