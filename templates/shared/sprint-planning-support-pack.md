# Sprint Planning Support Pack

> Produced ahead of Sprint Planning to help the team commit with confidence. Save the filled copy to `src/<engagement>/delivery/`.

## 1. Candidate backlog items
_The backlog items proposed for the upcoming Sprint._

## 2. Readiness check
_Whether each candidate meets the Definition of Ready._

## 3. Suggested Sprint Goal options
_Two or three Sprint Goal options the team can choose from._

## 4. Dependencies
_Cross-item, team, or external dependencies affecting the Sprint._

## 5. Risks
_Risks that could threaten the Sprint Goal or delivery._

## 6. Suggested task breakdown
_A draft decomposition of items into tasks for discussion._

## 7. QA work needed
_Testing effort and QA activities the Sprint will require._

## 8. DevOps work needed
_CI/CD, environment, or deployment work the Sprint will require._

## 9. Open questions
_Unresolved questions to clarify before or during planning._

## 10. Sprint confidence level
_An honest confidence rating in the Sprint plan, with reasoning._
